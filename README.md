# ScreenScape
ScreenScape turns a shared folder on your home network into a living photo frame on Android TV.  Connect to an SMB/Samba share (NAS, PC, or file server), choose a folder, and play images and videos as a full-screen slideshow. Use it from the app or set it as a system screen saver where your device allows.

Features:

Browse SMB shares on your local network
Slideshow with adjustable interval (1–60 seconds)
Photos and videos (large videos may be skipped for reliability)
Optional “play entire video” or short random clips
Folder label on screen
Free, no ads, no account required
All media stays on your network. ScreenScape does not upload your photos or videos to the cloud.

Data

  -- Stored on the device (DataStore) to connect to the user’s server
